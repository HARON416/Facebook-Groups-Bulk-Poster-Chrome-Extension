# Facebook Groups Bulk Poster

Bulk post to multiple Facebook groups from one polished, easy-to-use side panel.

- ✅ Load and review your joined Facebook groups before starting a posting queue
- ✅ Search, select, and manage groups from one organized workspace
- ✅ Keep selected groups separate from the remaining loaded groups
- ✅ Attach multiple images to create richer, more engaging posts
- ✅ Reuse your post content without copying and pasting it into every group
- ✅ Preserve spacing and formatting across queued posts
- ✅ Set a maximum number of posts so the queue stops automatically at your chosen limit
- ✅ Choose minimum and maximum interval times for a fresh randomized delay between posts
- ✅ Shuffle selected groups before posting for a less predictable queue order
- ✅ Optionally rewrite descriptions with Gemini for clearer, more polished listings
- ✅ Follow live progress, randomized countdowns, successful posts, failures, and remaining groups
- ✅ Pause, resume, or manually stop an active posting queue when needed
- ✅ Review transparent posting logs to understand what the extension is doing at every step
- ✅ Use a focused Facebook workflow with a clean, colorful, and streamlined interface
- ✅ Save time when sharing product listings, promotions, announcements, or community updates

## Important Note About Buy & Sell Groups

- ✅ The extension captures its posting template from the first post created through the Facebook interface
- ✅ Buy & Sell groups do not provide the standard “Write something” text area required for this initial capture
- ✅ If the first UI post opens in a Buy & Sell group and does not work, retry with another selected group until the first post uses a regular group that has the “Write something” area
- ✅ After the initial template is captured successfully, the extension can continue processing the posting queue

Designed for sellers, marketers, community managers, and teams who regularly share posts across multiple Facebook groups and want a cleaner, faster workflow.
